## Prioritized verified batches

1. **A — engine correctness:** remove timer-caused losses/extensions; preserve geometric line interactions; improve help pickup/leverage and distinguish spot-drop from limited match; repair spatial throw reaction; deterministic gameplay fixtures asserting actual events, positions and outcomes. Do not infer realistic football from browser passes.
2. **B — training and playbook:** bounded input replay/timeline and slow motion, clear mode limits, safe play validation/duplication and exact saved-play consistency. Keep incomplete match branches explicit.
3. **C — athlete/coach workflows:** prevent workout recognition-completion bypass; repair nested navigation and persistence disclosures; retain approved dark interfaces.
4. **D — readiness:** reconcile private-media policies, document owner setup/consent/review gates and known security findings; verify available CI/Preview against final commit. No unapproved service activation.

Do not perform an uncontrolled formation/coverage/3D/backend rewrite. A coherent verified batch is deliverable; the remaining roadmap above remains open. No athlete beta/public launch until both infrastructure/privacy and coaching gates pass. Final implementation evidence and remaining gaps are recorded in the quality-pass report rather than retroactively labeling this baseline complete.
